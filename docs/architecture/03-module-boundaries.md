# Module Boundaries

## Consolidated bounded contexts

The noun-level requirements are grouped to avoid dozens of tiny interdependent modules.

| Context / internal modules                                                                       | Responsibility and owned data                                                                                                                                         | Allowed dependencies and calls                                                                                                | Events / transactions / security / scale                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform & Governance: Organization, IAM, Approvals, Audit, Configuration                        | Company, Branch, branch assignments, users/roles/permissions, approval policies/decisions, audit, versioned legal/operational configuration                           | Incoming from every module through narrow policy/audit interfaces; outgoing only to party references and infrastructure       | Publishes scope/policy changes; approval decisions. Access changes and approvals are transactional; enhanced audit and low-volume strong consistency                                  |
| Parties: Party Registry, Owner Profile, Vendor Profile                                           | Company-level persons/organizations, contacts, identity/verification, owner/representative and vendor profiles                                                        | Depends on Organization for scope metadata; used by all contexts through PartyId and purpose-specific queries                 | Publishes PartyUpdated/OwnerVerified/VendorApproved; protects sensitive fields; company-wide master with scoped views                                                                 |
| Portfolio: Property, RentableSpace, Service Engagement                                           | Property/building, operating-branch history, RentableSpace hierarchy/configurations, availability facts, effective engagements and capabilities                       | Depends on Parties and Organization; called by Pipeline, Leasing, Operations, Finance, Master Lease                           | Publishes space/engagement events; partition/transfer/engagement activation are atomic; overlap and area integrity; high-read indexing/projections                                    |
| Commercial Pipeline: CRM & Marketing, Application & Reservation, Brokerage & Placement           | Leads/activities, listings, viewings, applications/screening, reservations, brokerage/placement deals and commissions requested                                       | Depends on Parties, Portfolio, Documents, Approvals; requests Finance postings; hands approved candidates to Leasing          | Publishes reservation/deal events; reservation and deal closure transactions lock space; screening/privacy controls; CRM tables scale by time/status                                  |
| Leasing & Contracts                                                                              | Lease terms/parties, versions, signed artifacts references, occupancy, waivers, amendments, renewals, extensions, holdover, termination                               | Depends on Parties, Portfolio, Documents, Approvals; requests Finance charges/deposits; emits lifecycle events                | Lease activation atomically validates exclusive possession; signed versions immutable; sensitive document access; lease/status indexes                                                |
| Finance: Billing, Payments, Accounting, Deposits, Owner Accounting, Expenses/Payables, Utilities | Charges/waivers, receipts/allocations, accounts/journals/periods/fiscal years, fund classifications, deposit ledger, owner ledgers/payouts, vendor bills, allocations | Accepts posting requests from business contexts; reads stable references/policy facts; uses Approvals and Parties payout data | Owns all postings/events. Payment posting, reversal, allocation, payout, deposits and allocations are atomic. Maker-checker and append-only ledgers; partitioning/archival for volume |
| Master Lease & Sublease                                                                          | Master contract business state, sublease relationships, parent/child economics and profitability inputs                                                               | Depends on Portfolio, Parties, Leasing contract primitives; requests Finance postings; consumes payment/accounting events     | Publishes master/sublease lifecycle; activation validates hierarchy/term; company economics; period projections for margin                                                            |
| Property Operations: Maintenance, Work Orders, Vendors, Inspections                              | Requests, triage, work orders, quotations, evidence, inspections, operational vendor performance                                                                      | Depends on Portfolio, Parties/Vendors, Service Engagement, Documents, Approvals; requests Finance expense/payable posting     | Publishes maintenance/work events; work approval and completion transactions; field/mobile workloads and media scaling                                                                |
| Content & Communications: Documents, Notifications                                               | File metadata/versions/access class/templates; notification intents, preferences, delivery attempts                                                                   | Called by domain modules via purpose interfaces; Notifications consumes events and Party contacts                             | File finalize is transactional around metadata after upload; delivery is asynchronous; private-by-default; object-store and queue scale                                               |
| Reporting & Read Models                                                                          | Rebuildable projections, dashboards, report definitions, issued snapshots/exports                                                                                     | Consumes outbox events and approved query contracts; never writes source modules                                              | Eventually consistent except issued financial snapshots; branch/object authorization applied; queued heavy reports                                                                    |

## Required noun-to-module mapping

- Identity & Access, Organization & Branches, Approvals, Audit -> Platform & Governance.
- Parties, Owners -> Parties; Vendors has party master here and operational behavior in Property Operations/Finance Payables.
- Property, Rentable Space, Service Engagement -> Portfolio.
- CRM, Listings, Viewings, Applications, Reservations, Brokerage -> Commercial Pipeline.
- Leasing -> Leasing & Contracts.
- Billing/Charges, Payments, Accounting, Owner Accounting, Deposits, Expenses, Utilities -> Finance.
- Master Lease/Sublease -> Master Lease & Sublease.
- Maintenance, inspections, vendor operations -> Property Operations.
- Documents, Notifications -> Content & Communications.
- Reporting -> Reporting & Read Models.

No module may write another module's owned tables. Cross-context references use identifiers and stable snapshots where historical reproduction requires them.
