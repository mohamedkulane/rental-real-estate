# Hagaha Isticmaalaha — Af-Soomaali

Hagahan wuxuu sharxayaa sida loo isticmaalo **Nidaamka Maamulka Kirada & Iibka Hantida** (RERMS) tallaabo-tallaabo. Waxaa loogu talagalay shaqaalaha shirkadda, maamulayaasha laamaha, iyo isticmaalayaasha cusub.

## 1. Bilowga

### 1.1 Galitaanka (Login)

1. Fur browser-kaaga oo tag bogga galitaanka.
2. Geli **email-kaaga** iyo **furaha sirta ah**.
3. Riix **Gal** / **Sign in**.
4. Haddii aad tahay milkiile ama kireyste portal, waxaa laguu wareejin doonaa portal-kaaga. Shaqaalaha waxaa lagu geeyaa dashboard-ka maamulka.

### 1.2 Doorashada Luqadda

- Kor ku yaal header-ka, dooro **EN** (Ingiriis) ama **SO** (Soomaali).
- Doorashada waxaa lagu kaydiyaa browser-kaaga; marka xigta ee aad soo gasho waa la xusuusan doonaa.
- Bogga galitaanka sidoo kale waxaad ka dooran kartaa luqadda ka hor intaadan gelin.

### 1.3 Qaab-dhismeedka Shaashadda

| Qayb | Sharaxaad |
|------|-----------|
| **Sidebar (dhinaca bidix)** | Liiska module-yada: Macaamiisha, Kaydka Hantida, Kirada, Iibka, iwm. |
| **Header (kor)** | Raadinta guud, Bilow Cusub, ogeysiisyada, laamta, luqadda, profile-ka. |
| **Dashboard** | Koob-ka-koob guud oo ku saabsan hawshaada. |
| **Bilow Cusub** | Hawlo degdeg ah oo la bilaabi karo (Lead cusub, hanti cusub, iwm). |

---

## 2. Macaamiisha (CRM)

Macmiil (**Lead**) waa qof ama urur soo jeeda oo doonaya adeeg: **kirada**, **iibsiga**, **iibinta**, ama **adeeg dhismo**.

### 2.1 Abuurista Macmiil Cusub

1. Sidebar → **Macaamiisha** → **Macaamiil Soo Jeeda** (Leads).
2. Riix **Add Lead** ama isticmaal **Bilow Cusub** → **Add Lead**.
3. Buuxi:
   - Magaca / xiriirka
   - **Ujeedda (Intent)**: Kirada, Iib, Iibiye, ama Dhismo
   - Laanta mas'uulka ka ah
4. Kaydi.

### 2.2 Doorbidyada Macmiilka (Preferences)

Marka macmiil la abuuro ama la cusboonaysiiyo, geli doorbidyada:

| Doorbid | Sharaxaad |
|---------|-----------|
| **Miisaaniyad** | Kirada ama qiimaha iibka (min/max) |
| **Nooca hanti** | Guri, ganacsi, dhismo, iwm. |
| **Qolalka** | Tirada qolalka jiifka (bedrooms) |
| **Aagga la doorbiday** | Magaalada, degmada, ama xaafadda |
| **Taariikhda** | Goorta uu doonayo inuu galo ama iibsado |

Doorbidyadan waxay saameeyaan **matching-ka** (liiska ku habboon).

### 2.3 Matching — Liiska Ku Habboon

**Matching** waa shaqo muhiim ah: system-ku wuxuu soo bandhigayaa listings ku habboon macmiilka.

#### Shuruudaha Matching

System-ku wuxuu hubiyaa:

1. **Ujeedda macmiilka**
   - Macmiil **kirada** doonaya → kaliya **Rental Listings**
   - Macmiil **iib** doonaya → kaliya **Sale Listings**
   - Macmiil **iibiye** ama **dhismo** → matching ma jiro

2. **Laamta**
   - Listing waa inuu ku jiraa laamta mas'uulka ka ah macmiilka

3. **Bannaanida (Availability) — MUHIIM**
   - **Kirada**: Goob kasta oo la kireeyo (**Rentable Space**) waa la hubiyaa in aanay heysan heshiis firfircoon ama ballan firfircoon
   - Hal guri wuxuu yeelan karaa goobo badan; mid la kireeyay **lama soo bandhigo** mar kale ilaa uu bannaan noqdo
   - **Iibka**: Hanti la iibiyay ama heshiis iib la aqbalay **lama soo bandhigo**

4. **Doorbidyada kale**
   - Miisaaniyad, nooca hanti, qolalka, aagga la doorbiday

#### Sida loo arko Matching

1. Tag **Macaamiil Soo Jeeda** → dooro macmiil.
2. Tab-ka **Matches** / **Liiska ku habboon**.
3. Arag liiska: lambarka listing-ka, goobta/hantida, qiimaha, sababaha loo doortay.

#### Sababaha Matching (Why it matched)

Tusaalooyin:

- Macmiilku wuxuu raadinayaa kirada
- Goobtan kirada waa bannaan (lama kireyn)
- Kiradu waxay ku jirtaa miisaaniyadda
- Goobtu waxay la mid tahay aagga la doortay

### 2.4 Pipeline & Raadraaca

- **Waddada Iibka (Pipeline)**: arag xaaladda macaamiisha (cusub, la xiriiray, viewing, iwm.)
- **Raadraaca (Follow-Ups)**: qor wicitaanada/xiriirada mustaqbalka

## 3. Kaydka Hantida (Portfolio)

### 3.1 Hantida (Properties)

1. **Portfolio** → **Hantida**
2. Abuur hanti cusub: magac, cinwaan, nooca, laamta
3. Ku xir **milkiile** (Owner)

### 3.2 Goobaha La Kireeyo (Rentable Spaces)

Hal **Property** wuxuu yeelan karaa goobo badan (qol, guri, dukaan, iwm.):

1. **Portfolio** → **Goobaha La Kireeyo**
2. Abuur goob cusub oo ku xiran hantida
3. Hubi in goobtu **ACTIVE** tahay ka hor inta aan listing la samayn

**Xeer muhiim ah:** Goob la kireeyay (heshiis firfircoon) lama ogola in mar kale la kireeyo ama la iibiyo ilaa heshiiska dhammaado ama la joojiyo.

### 3.3 Milkiilayaasha & Dadka

- **Milkiilayaasha**: diiwaanka milkiilayaasha hantida
- **Dadka / Qaybaha (Parties)**: dad iyo ururro (kireystayaal, alaab-qeybiyeyaal, iwm.)

### 3.4 Heshiisyada Adeegga (Service Agreements)

Ka hor inta listing la daabaco, waa in **Service Engagement** firfircoon jiraa:

- Dillaalinta kirada
- Maamul buuxa
- Iibinta hanti

## 4. Kirada (Rental)

### 4.1 Liiska Kirada (Rental Listings)

1. **Kirada** → **Liiska Kirada**
2. Abuur listing cusub:
   - Dooro **Rentable Space** bannaan
   - Ku xir Service Engagement
   - Geli qiimaha kirada, sharaxaad
3. Dhibic nolosha: **Draft** → **Review** → **Published**

**System-ku wuu diidaa** in listing la abuuro ama la daabaco haddii goobtu hore u kireysan tahay ama ballan firfircoon leedahay.

### 4.2 Hawlaha Kirada

| Hawl | Goob |
|------|------|
| Booqashooyin (Viewings) | CRM / Kirada |
| Codsiyada (Applications) | Kirada |
| Ballanqaadyada (Reservations) | Kirada |
| Heshiisyada Kirada (Leases) | Kirada |
| Kireystayaasha (Tenants) | Kirada |
| Geliista Guriga (Move-In) | Kirada |
| Dib-u-cusboonaysiinta (Renewals) | Kirada |

### 4.3 Dillaalinta Kirada & Maamul Buuxa

- **Dillaalinta Kirada**: heshiisyada dillaalinta
- **Maamul Buuxa**: hantida shirkaddu si buuxda u maamusho

## 5. Iibka (Sales)

### 5.1 Liiska Iibka (Sale Listings)

1. **Iibka** → **Liiska Iibka**
2. Abuur listing hanti **ACTIVE** ah oo aan la iibin
3. Daabac marka diyaar tahay

**System-ku wuu diidaa** hanti la iibiyay ama heshiis iib la aqbalay.

### 5.2 Hawlaha Iibka

- **Iibinta Hantida**: dashboard-ka iibka
- **Waddada Iibka**: raadraaca heshiisyada
- **Soo-jeedinta Iibka (Offers)**: dalabyada iibsiga
- **Xallinta Iibka (Settlements)**: dhammaynta iibka

---

## 6. Maaliyadda (Finance)

### 6.1 Biilka & Lacag-bixinta

1. **Maaliyadda** → **Biilka & Lacag-bixinta**
2. Arag koob-ka-koob: dalabyada, qaansheegyada, lacag-bixinnada

### 6.2 Hawlaha Maaliyadeed

| Hawl | Sharaxaad |
|------|-----------|
| **Jadwalka Biilka** | Goorta lacagta la dalbado |
| **Dalabyada Lacagta (Charges)** | Dalabyada kireystaha |
| **Qaansheegyada (Invoices)** | Warqadaha rasmiga ah |
| **Lacag-bixinnada (Payments)** | Lacagta la helay |
| **Bayaannada Milkiilaha** | Warbixinta milkiilaha |
| **Bixinta Milkiilaha (Payouts)** | Lacagta loo dirayo milkiilaha — waxay u baahan tahay ansixin |
| **Kharashyada (Expenses)** | Kharashyada hantida |
| **Xisaabinta (Accounting)** | Journal entries, xisaabinta |

### 6.3 Xeerarka Maaliyadeed (Muhiim)

- Diiwaannada la **post** gareeyay **lama tirtiro**
- Sixitaanka waxaa lagu sameeyaa **reversal** ama **adjustment**
- **Aaminaadda (Deposit)** waa deyn, ma aha dakhliga shirkadda
- Lacagta milkiilaha waa inay ka go'daan dakhliga shirkadda

## 14. Su'aalo Inta Badan La Isweydiiyo (FAQ)

### Matching ma muujinayo wax — maxaa sabab ah?

Hubi:

1. Macmiilku ma yahay **RENT** ama **BUY**?
2. Ma jiraan **Published** listings laamta macmiilka?
3. Goobaha kirada ma yihiin **bannaan** (aan heshiis firfircoon lahayn)?
4. Hantida iibka ma **lama iibin**?
5. Doorbidyada (miisaaniyad, aag) ma aad u xaddidan yihiin?

### Hal guri goobo badan buu leeyahay — mid la kireeyay ma saameeyaa kuwa kale?

Maya. Matching wuxuu hubiyaa **goob kasta si gooni ah**. Goob la kireeyay keliya ayaa laga saaraa; goobaha kale ee bannaan waa la soo bandhigi karaa.

### Sidee luqadda u beddelaa?

Header → **EN** / **SO**, ama profile menu → Luqadda.

### Ma tirtiri karaa lacag-bixin la post gareeyay?

Maya. Isticmaal **reversal** ama **adjustment** sida ku cad xeerarka maaliyadda.
