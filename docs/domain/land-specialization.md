# Land Specialization

Land is a physical Property/RentableSpace specialization, never a service model. A LAND RentableSpace requires permitted use and may store dimensions, current use, boundary description, road access, and fenced state. Area remains in effective RentableSpace measurements.

Land does not require or accept residential bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, living rooms, balconies, or furniture fields. Future maintenance behavior is controlled by ServiceEngagement and remains outside Phase 4.
